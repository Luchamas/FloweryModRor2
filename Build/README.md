# Flowery

![Flowery](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Luchamas/FloweryModRor2/main/Build/readme-icons/CharSelection.png)

**Flowery**, leader of the Flowers from DELTARUNE, joins the Planet as a playable survivor.

Sorry to keep you waiting!

## Skills

| | Skill | Description |
| :---: | --- | --- |
| ![TENSION](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Luchamas/FloweryModRor2/main/Build/readme-icons/Passive.png) | **TENSION**<br>*Passive* | Being near enemies builds **TP**, up to 100%. Landing hits and dashing through enemies builds it faster. |
| ![Flower Punches](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Luchamas/FloweryModRor2/main/Build/readme-icons/Primary.png) | **Flower Punches**<br>*Primary* | Throw alternating punches for **230%** damage. A punch that connects heals you for **1%** of your max health. Hold to keep swinging. |
| ![Jarona](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Luchamas/FloweryModRor2/main/Build/readme-icons/Jarona.png) | **Jarona**<br>*Secondary* | Instantly dash about 15m, breaking through enemies for **200%** damage and stunning them. Holds **2** charges. |
| ![Here I Come San Frandisco!](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Luchamas/FloweryModRor2/main/Build/readme-icons/HereICome.png) | **Here I Come San Frandisco!**<br>*Utility* | Hang in the air for **0.8s**, then launch about 26m through everything for **300%** damage, stunning. |
| ![OMEGA FLOWERY](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Luchamas/FloweryModRor2/main/Build/readme-icons/OmegaFlowery.png) | **OMEGA FLOWERY**<br>*Special* | Requires **75% TP**. Rise into the air, untouchable, and erupt in vines for **400%** damage plus **8%** per TP. Then become **OMEGA**: fly freely wherever you look, deal **25%** more damage, and your special becomes **LAST JARONA**. OMEGA lasts until your TP drains to 0%, up to 20 seconds on a full bar. |
| ![LAST JARONA](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Luchamas/FloweryModRor2/main/Build/readme-icons/LastJarona.png) | **LAST JARONA**<br>*Special, while OMEGA* | Wind up for **0.8s**, then dive into the first enemy in your path for **600%** damage and detonate for **1200%** in a wide blast. Explodes at the end of the dive even if it hits nothing. |

## Taunts

| Keys | Taunt |
| --- | --- |
| **Ctrl + 1** | Flowery flips his hair back and offers you his palm. |
| **Ctrl + 2** | HERE I COME SAN FRANDISCO! |
| **Ctrl + 3** | He winds up a Jarona... then shrugs and drifts backwards instead. |

Moving, jumping or using a skill cancels a taunt. Rebind the keys in `BepInEx/config/com.deltarune.FloweryMod.cfg`.
